OpenSL1

This installer sets up a free CoreAudio driver for the Rane SL1 (Serato Scratch Live). Afterwards the SL1 shows up as the audio device “Rane SL1” with 4 inputs (Input 1/2) and 4 outputs (Line 1/2), for example for Mixxx with timecode vinyl.

The driver is copied to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL/OpenSL1.driver. The macOS audio server restarts afterwards, so running audio apps will drop out for a moment.

OpenSL1 is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Rane, Serato or inMusic.
